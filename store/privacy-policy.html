Privacy Policy — FoodLens AI

Last updated: 23 October 2026

FoodLens AI is a food and nutrition journal. This policy explains what the app collects, where it goes, and how to get rid of it. It describes the app as it is actually built.

What the app collects

Your account. An email address and a password, so your journal can follow you to another phone. Passwords are handled by our authentication provider and are never visible to us.

Your profile. A display name, if you give one, and a profile picture, if you choose one.

What you log. The meals and foods you record, their portions and times, and the water and weight you enter.

Your preferences. Your goal and daily targets, dietary patterns, any allergens you choose to tell us about, the ingredients in your kitchen list, and your choice of units.

What you create. Recipes you generate or save, and your shopping lists.

A record of AI use. For each photo analysis or recipe generation we store the outcome, which model answered, how long it took, and the size of what was sent. This is what enforces the daily limits. It does not contain the photo.

What the app does not collect

The app contains no analytics, advertising or tracking software of any kind. There is no third-party SDK watching how you use it.

It does not ask for or collect your location, contacts, calendar, or microphone. It does not build an advertising profile, and it does not sell or share your data for advertising. Nothing is sold to anyone.

Photographs

This is the part worth reading carefully.

When you photograph a meal, the image is sent to Google's Gemini API so it can be identified. Google processes the image to answer that request.

The photo is not kept on our servers unless you turn on Keep my meal photos in the app. That setting is off by default. With it off, the image is used for the analysis and not stored. With it on, the photo is saved to your own private folder, readable only by your account, and turning the setting back off deletes the photos already kept.

Photos you take are also kept on your own phone so the app can show them to you. Those never leave the device.

The camera permission is used for photographing meals and reading barcodes. The photo library permission is used only when you pick an existing picture.

Where your data is stored

On servers operated by Supabase in Mumbai, India (ap-south-1).

Every table is protected so that your rows are readable only by your own account. This is enforced by the database itself, not only by the app.

Deleting your data

In the app: Profile → Delete my account. This deletes your account and the data stored against it, including any kept photos.

On the device only: Profile → Delete everything on this device clears the local copy without touching your account.

If you cannot reach the app, email the address at the top of this policy and ask for your account to be deleted.

Children

FoodLens AI is not directed at children and is not intended for anyone under 13. We do not knowingly collect data from children. If you believe a child has given us data, email us and we will delete it.

Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to ask for a copy of your data, to correct it, or to have it deleted. Email the address above and we will respond.

Changes

If this policy changes, the date at the top changes with it, and the new version replaces this page.
