## Full description (4000 characters max)

```
FoodLens AI is a food journal that does the counting for you.

Photograph what you are about to eat and the app identifies the foods and
estimates the calories, protein, carbohydrate and fat. Or look a food up in
a measured nutrition database, scan the barcode on a packet, or simply type
what you had. Four ways in, because no single one works for every meal.

WHERE EVERY NUMBER COMES FROM

Most trackers show you a number and leave you to trust it. This one records
where each figure came from and shows it beside the food: measured from a
database, read off a packet's label, estimated by AI, or entered by you.
When it is an estimate, it says so, and tells you how confident it is.

That matters because an estimate from a photograph is not the same thing as
a figure from a laboratory, and an app that blurs the two is not being
honest with you.

WHAT YOU CAN DO

• Photograph a meal and get a breakdown of what is in it
• Search a measured nutrition database of real food records
• Scan a barcode and read the manufacturer's own figures
• Describe a meal in your own words
• Track water and weight alongside what you eat
• Save meals you eat often and add them again in one tap
• Get a daily score worked out from your own entries, with the reasoning shown
• Build recipes from what is actually in your kitchen
• Turn a recipe into a shopping list

NO COACH, NO GUESSWORK DRESSED AS ADVICE

The daily score is arithmetic over the meals you logged. Tap any insight and
it shows which entries produced it. There is no advice generated about your
health, and nothing is invented to fill an empty screen.

WORKS WITHOUT A SIGNAL

Meals are saved on your phone first and sync when you are back online. The
journal does not stop working on a train.

YOUR DATA

No advertising. No analytics. No tracking of any kind - there is not a
single tracking library in this app.

Meal photographs are sent for analysis and are not stored unless you turn on
"Keep my meal photos", which is off by default. Turning it back off deletes
the ones already saved, on your phone and in your account.

You can delete your account and everything in it from inside the app.

The only permission the app asks for is the camera, used to photograph meals
and read barcodes.

NUTRITION ESTIMATES ARE ESTIMATES

FoodLens AI is not a medical device and does not give medical or dietary
advice. Figures from a photograph are estimates and should be treated as
such. Talk to a qualified professional about decisions that matter.
```
